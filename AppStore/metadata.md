## Description (4000)
Say exactly what you mean. Daily Word gives you one new word or phrase every day, tells you where it came from, and helps it stick with quick reviews right on your home screen. Free forever.

It's built for words you'd actually use in conversation: ways to describe how you feel, what people are like, how a discussion is going, and phrases that make you sound clear and considered. Not exam lists, not obscure trivia.

EVERY DAY, A NEW WORD
Open the app or glance at the widget. You get the word, how to say it, what it means, and a sentence someone would really say.

KNOW WHERE IT CAME FROM
Every entry explains its origin. Learn that "quarantine" means forty days, that "sycophant" literally means "fig-shower", and that the popular story behind "rule of thumb" is a myth. Words that share a root are one tap away.

REVIEW WITHOUT OPENING THE APP
The Review widget shows a past word. Tap to see the meaning, then tell it whether you knew it. Words you know well come back rarely; words you miss come back tomorrow. This is spaced repetition, the most reliable way to make words stick.

ADD ANY WORD YOU MEET
Heard a word you liked? Look it up in the app. Daily Word fetches the definition and origin, you adjust anything you want, and it joins your reviews.

ON YOUR IPHONE, IPAD AND MAC
Your progress and your own words sync privately through your iCloud account.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 700+ hand-picked words and phrases with origins
• Home screen, desktop and lock screen widgets
• Pronunciation you can hear
• A library of every word you've learned

FREE, FOREVER
No price, no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases, now or later. Daily Word is open source, so it can never be taken away from you.

PRIVATE
No account and no tracking. Daily Word collects nothing.

## Notes for App Review
Daily Word works without an account. A new word appears on the Today tab each day. Past words become due for review the day after you first see them, so on a fresh install the Review tab says "Nothing to review" until tomorrow. To try reviewing straight away: open Library → + → type "serendipity" → Look up → Add. That word is reviewed tomorrow too, but you can see the flow. Widgets: add "Word of the Day" and "Review" from the widget gallery.
