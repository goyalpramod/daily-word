# Daily Word

One new word or phrase every day, with the story of where it came from. Review past words right from your home screen. Free forever, open source, and private.

## Help

**Where's today's word?** On the Today tab and in the *Word of the Day* widget. It changes at midnight.

**Why is Review empty?** A word becomes due the day after you first see it. Words you know well come back less and less often; words you miss come back the next day.

**How do I add the widgets?** iPhone and iPad: touch and hold the home screen, tap **+**, search *Daily Word*. Mac: right-click the desktop, choose **Edit Widgets**, search *Daily Word*.

**How do I add my own word?** Library → **+** → type the word → **Look up** → **Add**.

**My progress isn't syncing.** Make sure every device is signed in to the same iCloud account, then open the app on each one.

**Found a wrong definition or origin?** Please [open an issue](https://github.com/goyalpramod/daily-word/issues). The word list is open source and corrections are welcome.
