# Privacy policy

*Last updated: November 24, 2026*

Daily Word does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

**Your progress and the words you add** are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also synced through your own private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. Only you can access that data. The developer cannot see it.

**Looking up a word** sends that word, and nothing else, to two public dictionary services to fetch its definition and origin:
[dictionaryapi.dev](https://dictionaryapi.dev) and [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org) (Wikimedia Foundation).
Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

**The bonus word** is read from Wiktionary's public Word of the Day page. No information about you is sent.

Questions: open an issue at [github.com/goyalpramod/daily-word](https://github.com/goyalpramod/daily-word/issues).
